This page lists the sightings of non-indigenous species you have reported to
MAMIAS. Each report is checked by the MAMIAS team before it is published.
Here is how to report a sighting and follow it through review.

[TOC]

## Your reports at a glance

![My Species Reports, with its counters and five reports](/images/docs/species-reports/01-page.png)

The four counters at the top give your **Total** reports and how many are
**Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**.

Each row shows the **species**, the report's **status**, a small map of where
you saw it, the **depth**, when you **observed** it and when you **submitted**
it. The newest reports come first. The pin takes the status's colour: green
approved, gray pending, red rejected. On a narrow screen the less important
columns are left out.

- **Pending Review**: waiting for the MAMIAS team.
- **Approved**: accepted and published. It appears on the species' page in the
  [NIS data explorer](/pages/data).
- **Rejected**: not accepted. Hover the status to read the reviewer's reason
  (see [If a report is not accepted](#guide-if-a-report-is-not-accepted)).

## Report a sighting

Click **Report New Occurrence**.

![The report form](/images/docs/species-reports/02-form.png)

1. **Species** (required). Type at least three letters of the scientific name.
   **Country** above it is optional: it narrows the list to species recorded in
   that country.
2. **Depth (m)**, **Abundance (density)** and **Habitats**: as much as you know.
   Abundance uses the ACFOR scale (Abundant, Common, Frequent, Occasional,
   Rare): how tightly packed the species was where you saw it.
3. **Date & Time of Observation** (required): when you saw it, not when you
   report it.
4. **Extent (area or count)**: how much there was in total, as an area in m² or
   a number of individuals. Once you enter it, say which unit and whether you
   **measured** or **estimated** it.
5. **Location** tab. Click the map where you made the sighting; click again to
   move the pin. Zoom in for precision, and put the pin in the sea where you
   were diving or fishing, not on the shore.
6. **Photos** tab. Add pictures that show the species clearly: JPEG, PNG or
   WebP, up to 5 MB each. They are the reviewers' best evidence.
7. **Notes** tab. Anything that helps: how you identified it, what it was doing,
   who was with you.
8. Click **Submit**.

The report appears in your list as *Pending Review*, and the MAMIAS team is
notified.

### The species is not in the list

Only species already in the MAMIAS catalogue can be reported. If yours is
missing, click **Not listed?** above the Species field: it opens
[My Species Suggestions](/my-suggestions) in a new tab, where you can propose
it as a new non-indigenous species.

## Read the reviewer's decision

You receive an email when your report is reviewed. An approval can carry a
note from the reviewer; a rejection always gives the reason.

Click **View** on any report to see everything you sent and its review: when
you submitted it, its status and the reviewer's notes.

![A report's details](/images/docs/species-reports/03-view.png)

## Change or withdraw a report

While a report is **Pending Review**:

- **Edit** opens the form again. Correct anything and click **Save changes**;
  the report stays in the review queue.
- **Withdraw** removes it from the queue. This cannot be undone.

An **approved** report can no longer be changed.

## If a report is not accepted

![The reason for a rejection, on hover](/images/docs/species-reports/04-rejected.png)

Hover the *Rejected* status to read the reason. Common ones are a pin on land,
a photo that shows a native look-alike, or a sighting already reported.

You can fix the report and send it back: click **Revise & resubmit**. The
reviewer's feedback is shown at the top of the form. Make the changes it asks
for, then click **Resubmit for review**. The report returns to *Pending
Review*, and the reviewers see that it was resubmitted and why it was rejected
the first time.

![Revising a rejected report](/images/docs/species-reports/05-resubmit.png)
