MAMIAS (Marine Mediterranean Invasive Alien Species) is the regional database of
non-indigenous marine species recorded in the Mediterranean Sea, run by SPA/RAC
for UNEP/MAP. Anyone can browse it; with a free account you can also contribute.

## What you can do

| You can | Without an account | With an account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browse the species catalogue and each species' page | Yes | Yes |

Everything contributors send is checked by the MAMIAS team before it appears in
the database.

## Browsing without an account

The whole database can be read without signing in. Use a computer or a tablet:
on a phone-sized screen the site currently shows a notice asking for a larger
screen.

**Home page (/).** An overview of MAMIAS with links to everything below. Its
**MAMIAS at a glance** block sums up the Mediterranean dashboard: how many
non-indigenous species are reported, how many per EcAp sub-region (click one to
see it on the map), how established they are, the main groups and the latest
arrivals.

![The home page](/images/docs/manuals/user-02-home.png)

**Data explorer (/pages/data).** Every non-indigenous species in the catalogue,
one row each:

![The data explorer](/images/docs/manuals/user-03-data-explorer.png)

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| NIS Scientific Name | The species, as accepted by WoRMS |
| 1st Year of Introduction | Year of its first record in the Mediterranean |
| 1st Country of Introduction | Country of that first record |
| NIS Status | Whether it counts as introduced (NIS), cryptogenic, questionable, a range expansion or data deficient |
| Establishment Status | Whether it reproduces there: established, invasive, casual, and so on |

Type in **Search** to find a species by name or family. **Filters** above the table narrow
it by year range, country, statuses, EcAp sub-region and introduction pathway:
pick them, then click **Apply filters**. Click a row, or its **View data**
button, to open the species. The page's **Guide** button explains it step by step.

**Species page (/pages/data/…).** Three parts. First, three tabs on one row: the
**species** itself (its name and authority as the tab title, then its
classification, identifiers and statuses; the Aphia ID links to WoRMS),
**Synonyms** and **References**. Then the **Introduction event** (first record,
statuses and the reference that documents it, with tabs for sub-regions,
countries and pathways), and **Occurrences**, a map of every approved sighting.

![A species page](/images/docs/manuals/user-04-species.png)

**Map (/pages/map).** The species on a map of the Mediterranean, either by EcAp
subregion (shaded by the number of species) or by country of first record (one
bubble per country, at its centre or near its coast, sized by the number of species; no national
boundaries are drawn). Click an area to see its summary (statuses, main groups, latest
arrivals) and its species. The data explorer's filters sit above the map and
apply at once; tick **Occurrences** to pin the approved sightings. The page's
**Guide** button explains it step by step.

![The map, a subregion picked](/images/docs/map/02-subregion.png)

**Dashboards.** *Mediterranean dashboard* gives the basin-wide picture: how many
species, since when, where, by which pathway, in which groups. *Dashboard by
country* gives the same for one country. Both count only validated
non-indigenous species, following the baseline of Galanidi et al. (2023).

![The Mediterranean dashboard](/images/docs/manuals/user-05-dashboard.png)

## Your account

An account is free and takes two minutes. You need one only to contribute.

### Create an account

1. Open **/login** and click **sign up for an account**.
2. Fill in your title, first and last name, country, email address and a
   password, then tick the human check.
3. Click **Sign up**. MAMIAS emails you a confirmation link.
4. Click **Visit MAMIAS** in that email to confirm your address. Until you do,
   you can sign in but not contribute.

![The sign-up form](/images/docs/manuals/user-06-sign-up.png)

No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder, then use the link on the
confirmation screen to send it again.

### Sign in and out

Sign in at **/login** with your email and password. You land on the home page;
your name at the top right opens your menu:

![Your menu](/images/docs/manuals/user-07-user-menu.png)

- **Public Profile**
- **My Bibliographic references**
- **My Species Reports**
- **My Species Suggestions**
- **Log out**

Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow
the emailed link.

### Your profile

**Public Profile** holds what the MAMIAS team knows about you:

![Your profile](/images/docs/manuals/user-08-profile.png)

- **Personal details:** title, name, phone (a button checks whether it is on
  WhatsApp), country.
- **Account:** email address and password. A new email address must be
  confirmed again.
- **Professional focus:** the taxonomic groups you know, a short biography, and
  the EcAp sub-regions and countries you work in. It helps the team know whom to
  ask about a record.

Click **Save Data** when you are done.

## Contributing

There are three ways to add to MAMIAS, each with its own page in your menu. Pick
by what you have in hand:

| You have | Use | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A publication recording a non-indigenous species | My Bibliographic references | Add New Reference |
| A sighting of a species MAMIAS already lists | My Species Reports | Report New Occurrence |
| A non-indigenous species MAMIAS does not list yet | My Species Suggestions | Suggest New NIS |

Each page shows your submissions with their status (*Pending Review*,
*Approved*, *Rejected*) and counters at the top. Each has a **Guide** button with
illustrated, step-by-step help; the summary below is enough to start.

![My Species Reports](/images/docs/species-reports/01-page.png)

### Add a reference

Paste the publication's **DOI**: the title, authors, year and type are filled in
from Crossref. Check them, attach the PDF if you have it (10 MB at most), and
submit. If MAMIAS already holds the publication, the form says so under the DOI.
A rejection is final for references, but you can discuss any reference with the
reviewers through its **Discussion** badge.

![The DOI step of a new reference](/images/docs/references/02-doi-step.png)

### Report a sighting

Choose the **Species** (type three letters; pick a country first to narrow the
list), the **date and time** you saw it, and click the map where you saw it, in
the sea, not on the shore. Add depth, abundance (the ACFOR scale), habitats, how
much there was, photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP, 5 MB each) and notes as far as you
know them. While a report is pending you can **Edit** or **Withdraw** it. If it is
rejected, hover the status for the reason, then **Revise & resubmit**. Species
not in the list? Click **Not listed?** above the Species field.

![The sighting report form](/images/docs/species-reports/02-form.png)

### Suggest a species

Type at least four letters of the **scientific name**: names come from WoRMS,
and *[verified]* marks the accepted one. Authority and Aphia ID fill in by
themselves. Add photos (required) and link the publications that record the
species in the Mediterranean. When approved, the species joins the catalogue as
a draft for the team to complete. If rejected, **Resubmit** sends a copy back
with whatever you add. The **Discussion** in a suggestion's details is the place
to talk with the team; replies are not emailed, so look back for them.

![The species suggestion form](/images/docs/suggestions/02-form.png)

## Emails, privacy and terms

MAMIAS emails you only about your own account and submissions:

| Email | When | Its button opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirm your address | You sign up or change your email | MAMIAS, confirming the address |
| Password reset | You click Forgot password? | The new-password page |
| Your reference was reviewed | A reviewer approves or rejects a reference | My Bibliographic references |
| New comment on your reference | A reviewer writes in its Discussion | The reference, to reply |
| Your occurrence report was approved / not accepted | A reviewer decides on a sighting | My Species Reports |
| Your species suggestion was approved / not accepted | A reviewer decides on a suggestion | — |

Buttons in MAMIAS emails open the site in a normal browser tab.

**Cookies.** On your first visit a dialog asks for your choice: **Accept all**
or **Reject all**. The site stays dimmed until you answer. To change your mind
later, use **Change Cookie Preferences** at the bottom of any page. The
**Cookies policy** page lists each cookie, why it is set and for how long.

![The cookie dialog on a first visit](/images/docs/manuals/user-01-cookies.png)

**Terms.** The **Terms of use** page sets the conditions for using MAMIAS and
contributing records. MAMIAS data may be reused under the CC BY 4.0 licence:
credit MAMIAS as the source. The **Legal notice** names the publisher and host.

## Questions and problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page is dimmed and nothing responds to clicks | Answer the cookie dialog (Accept all or Reject all) |
| The site asks for a larger screen | Use a computer or tablet; phone screens are not supported yet |
| No confirmation email after signing up | Check spam, then resend it from the confirmation screen |
| I can sign in but not open My Species Reports | Confirm your email address first |
| I forgot my password | **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page |
| My species is not in the report form | It is not catalogued yet: suggest it with **Not listed?** |
| My report was rejected | Hover its status for the reason, then **Revise & resubmit** |
| My reference was rejected | Rejection is final; ask the reviewers in its **Discussion** if you disagree |
| A map stays grey | The map server is briefly unreachable; reload the page later |

For anything else, write to the MAMIAS team at the contact address given on the
**Legal notice** page.
