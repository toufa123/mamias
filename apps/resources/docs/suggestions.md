This page lists the species you have proposed for the MAMIAS catalogue. Suggest
a species when you have found a non-indigenous species in the Mediterranean that
MAMIAS does not list yet. The MAMIAS team checks each suggestion before adding
the species.

To report a sighting of a species MAMIAS already lists, use
[My Species Reports](/my-species-reports) instead.

## Your suggestions at a glance

![My Species Suggestions, with its counters and three suggestions](/images/docs/suggestions/01-page.png)

The four counters at the top give your **Total** suggestions and how many are
**Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**.

Each row shows the suggested **scientific name**, its **authority**, the
**status**, a small map of where it was found and when you **submitted** it.
The newest suggestions come first.

- **Pending Review**: waiting for the MAMIAS team.
- **Approved**: accepted. The species has been added to the MAMIAS catalogue.
- **Rejected**: not accepted. Hover the status to read the reason (see
  [If a suggestion is not accepted](#guide-if-a-suggestion-is-not-accepted)).

## Suggest a species

Click **Suggest New NIS**.

![The suggestion form](/images/docs/suggestions/02-form.png)

1. **Scientific Name** (required). Type at least four letters: the names come
   from [WoRMS](https://www.marinespecies.org), the World Register of Marine
   Species. *[verified]* after a name means WoRMS accepts it; prefer that
   name over a synonym. **Authority** and **Aphia ID** (the WoRMS identifier)
   are filled in for you.
2. **Photos** tab (required). Add pictures that show the species clearly:
   JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each.
3. **Notes** tab. Under **Bibliographic References**, pick the publications
   that record the species in the Mediterranean, if any. A reference that is
   not in MAMIAS yet can be added from there with **+**: paste its DOI and the
   details are filled in.
4. Click **Submit**.

The suggestion appears in your list as *Pending Review*.

## Read the reviewer's decision

You receive an email when your suggestion is reviewed. Click **View** on any
suggestion to see what you sent and its review.

At the bottom, **Discussion** is where you and the MAMIAS team talk about the
suggestion: ask a question, add information, or answer the reviewer. Messages
are not emailed, so open the suggestion again to see replies.

![A suggestion's details](/images/docs/suggestions/03-view.png)

An approved species is added to the catalogue as a draft. The MAMIAS team then
completes it with data from WoRMS and other sources before it is published.

## Change a suggestion

While a suggestion is **Pending Review**, **Edit** opens the form again.
Correct anything and save; the suggestion stays in the review queue. Once it is
approved or rejected it can no longer be edited.

## If a suggestion is not accepted

![The reason for a rejection, on hover](/images/docs/suggestions/04-rejected.png)

Hover the *Rejected* status to read the reason. Common ones are a species that
is native to the Mediterranean, one already in the catalogue, or not enough
evidence that it was found here.

If you think the decision is wrong, say so in the suggestion's **Discussion**.

If you can answer the reason, for example with a better photo or a publication,
click **Resubmit**. A new suggestion with the same details is created and goes
back to *Pending Review*; the rejected one stays in your list. Edit the new one
to add what was missing.
