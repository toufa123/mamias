The map (**Explore MAMIAS › MAP**, /pages/map) shows where the non-indigenous
species in MAMIAS were recorded: by EcAp subregion, or by the country where each
was first recorded in the Mediterranean. Pick an area on the map to see its
summary and its species. Anyone can use it; no account is needed.

## The page at a glance

![Filters above the map of the EcAp subregions](/images/docs/map/01-page.png)

The page has four blocks, from top to bottom:

1. **Filters.** The same filters as the data explorer (/pages/data), in the same
   order. They narrow everything below them.
2. **The map.** The EcAp subregions, or the countries of first record.
3. **The summary** of the area you picked.
4. **The species** of that area, in a table.

Click the title bar of the filters, the map or the table to fold that block
away, and again to open it. **Guide** at the top right opens this page.

## Filters

Every filter applies as soon as you change it. There is no button to press:
the map, the summary and the table all redraw together.

| Filter | Keeps the species… |
| --- | --- |
| 1st Year of Introduction | first recorded in the Mediterranean between the two years |
| 1st Country of Introduction | first recorded in one of the countries you pick |
| NIS Status | with one of the statuses you pick (NIS, cryptogenic, …) |
| Establishment Status | established, casual, invasive, …, across the Mediterranean |
| EcAp Subregion | recorded in one of the subregions you pick |
| Present in Country | recorded in one of the countries you pick |
| CBD Pathway Category, Subcategory, Type, Uncertainty | introduced by the pathway you pick |

**Reset**, in the filters' title bar, clears them all.

NIS Status and Establishment Status are the species' status for the whole
Mediterranean. The summary's **Establishment** block shows its status inside the
subregion you picked, so the two can differ: a species casual basin-wide can be
established in one subregion.

![The EcAp filter narrowing the map to the Adriatic Sea](/images/docs/map/05-filtered.png)

**EcAp Subregion** also changes the map: only the subregions you pick are
counted and shaded; the others turn grey and cannot be picked. If the subregion
you had picked is filtered out, the first one you ticked is picked instead.

## The map

Drag the map to move, use **+** and
**−** (or the mouse wheel) to zoom. Hover
an area to read its name and number of species.

Two buttons in the map's title bar switch what it shows:

- **Subregions.** The four EcAp subregions of the Barcelona Convention
  (UNEP/MAP): Western Mediterranean, Adriatic Sea, Ionian Sea and Central
  Mediterranean, and Aegean-Levantine Sea. The darker the sea, the more
  species are recorded there (the scale is under the map).
- **Countries.** One bubble per country where species were first recorded in
  the Mediterranean, at the centre of the country, or near its Mediterranean
  coast when the centre lies far inland; the bigger the bubble, the
  more species, and the number is written inside the larger ones. Following
  Barcelona Convention practice the map draws no national boundaries. A
  species first recorded in two countries at once counts in both.

![A subregion picked, with its summary](/images/docs/map/02-subregion.png)

**Click a subregion or a bubble** to pick it. A picked subregion is outlined
in dark, a picked bubble turns dark and ripples, and the summary and the table
below switch to it. The page remembers the layer and the
area you picked in its address, so a link to it opens the same view.

### Occurrence pins

Tick **Occurrences** in the map's title bar to pin every approved sighting of
the species the filters keep, on either layer. Click a pin for its date,
coordinates, depth, abundance and habitats. Sightings are reported by MAMIAS
contributors and checked by the MAMIAS team before they appear.

![Countries of first record, with the occurrence pins](/images/docs/map/04-countries.png)

## The summary

Under the map, the area you picked:

- **The area** and its number of species. For a subregion, species recorded
  there; for a country, species first recorded there. **View these species**
  jumps to the table.
- **Establishment.** How many of those species are established, casual,
  invasive and so on: in that subregion for a subregion, across the
  Mediterranean for a country.
- **Top phyla.** The five groups with the most species there.
- **Most recent arrivals.** The five latest first records: the year a species
  first reached the subregion, or its first year in the Mediterranean for a
  country. Click a name to open the species.

## The species table

![The species of the Western Mediterranean](/images/docs/map/03-table.png)

The table lists the species of the area you picked, with the filters applied,
and its title says which area that is. It has the same columns as the data
explorer:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| NIS Scientific Name | The species, as accepted by WoRMS, with its authority |
| 1st Year of Introduction | Year of its first record in the Mediterranean |
| 1st Country of Introduction | Country of that first record |
| NIS Status | NIS, cryptogenic, questionable, range expansion or data deficient |
| Establishment Status | Established, invasive, casual, and so on |

Type in **Search** to find a species by name, and click a column title to sort
by it. Click a row, or **View data**, to open the species page: its catalogue
entry, its introduction event and a map of its sightings.
