This page lists the bibliographic references you have submitted to MAMIAS.
Each one is checked by the MAMIAS team before it can be cited in the database.
Here is how to add a reference and follow it through review.

[TOC]

## Your references at a glance

![My Bibliographic References, with two banners and four references](/images/docs/references/01-page.png)

Each row shows the reference's **code** (e.g. *mamias000005*, its permanent
identifier in MAMIAS), **short reference**, **type**, **year** and **status**:

- **Pending Review**: waiting for the MAMIAS team.
- **Approved**: accepted. It can now be cited in the database.
- **Rejected**: not accepted. The reviewer's reason is attached (see
  [If a reference is not accepted](#guide-if-a-reference-is-not-accepted)).

A speech bubble next to a status means the reviewer left a comment. Click the
status to read it.

## Add a reference

### Step 1: the DOI

1. Click **Add New Reference**.
2. Paste the reference's **DOI**. A link (`https://doi.org/10.…`),
   `doi:10.…` or the bare DOI all work.
3. Click outside the field. The title, authors, year and type are fetched
   from Crossref automatically. The **↻** button fetches them again.
4. Click **Next**.

![The DOI step of the form](/images/docs/references/02-doi-step.png)

No DOI? Leave the field empty and click **Next**. You will fill in the
reference by hand.

### Step 2: check the details

![The details step, filled in from the DOI](/images/docs/references/03-details-step.png)

1. Check what was filled in and correct anything that is wrong:
   - **Short reference**: how the reference is cited in the text, e.g.
     *Zenetos et al., 2012*.
   - **Resource type**: article, technical report, thesis, book or conference
     proceedings.
   - **Year** of publication.
   - **Full reference / title**: the complete citation.
2. Attach the **PDF** if you have it (10 MB maximum). It helps the reviewers
   when the article is not open access.
3. Click **Submit**.

The reference appears in your list as *Pending Review*, and the MAMIAS team is
notified.

### Already in MAMIAS?

![A DOI that is already in MAMIAS](/images/docs/references/04-already-in-mamias.png)

If you see *Already in MAMIAS as mamias000…* under the DOI, the reference is
already in the database and there is no need to submit it again. The same
applies to *Looks like mamias000…* under the full reference: a very similar
reference exists, so check it's not the same publication before submitting.

## Read the reviewer's decision

You receive an email and a notification when your reference is reviewed.

- **Click the status** to read the reviewer's comment.

  ![The reviewer's comment on an approved reference](/images/docs/references/05-reviewer-comment.png)

- **Click View** to see the whole reference and its review: when you submitted
  it, when it was reviewed and by whom (the reviewer's role), and the comment.

  ![A reference and its review](/images/docs/references/06-view.png)

Reviewers sometimes correct small details (a year, a typo) before approving.
The comment then says what was changed.

## Talk to the reviewers

Click the **Discussion** badge on any reference to ask a question or add
information. The number is how many messages the discussion holds. An amber
badge means there are new replies since your last message, and a banner at the
top of the page lists those references. Replying clears it.

![A new reply waiting: the Discussion badge is amber](/images/docs/references/07-discussion-badge.png)

![A discussion between the submitter and a reviewer](/images/docs/references/08-discussion.png)

Write your message in the box at the top. Your earlier messages can be edited
or deleted with the icons on the right.

## If a reference is not accepted

![The reviewer's reason for a rejection](/images/docs/references/09-rejected-reason.png)

A banner at the top of the page tells you when a reference was not accepted,
and **Show them** lists those references. Click the *Rejected* status to read
the reason.

A rejection is final, so a rejected reference cannot be sent again. Common
reasons are:

- the publication is outside MAMIAS's scope (not about non-indigenous species,
  or not about the Mediterranean);
- it is not a usable source (for example a press article);
- it is already in MAMIAS.

If you think the decision is wrong, or you have more information, say so in
the reference's **Discussion**.

## Find a reference

![The filters, by status, type and year](/images/docs/references/10-filters.png)

- **Search** looks in the code, the short reference and the DOI.
- The **filter** button narrows the list by status, type or year. Choose, then click
  **Apply filters**. **Reset** clears them.
- Click a column heading to sort by it.
